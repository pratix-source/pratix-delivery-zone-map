Free delivery zone map generator for local teams

Pratix Delivery & Service Area Map helps restaurants, pharmacies, couriers, field services and local retailers visualize where they serve customers. Draw one or more radius zones, label each area and export a lightweight map without uploading an address list.

What can you use it for?

  • Plan delivery fees and service territories for restaurants and online shops.
  • Explain coverage areas to customers with a simple visual map.
  • Compare nearby service zones before opening a new location.
  • Prepare a private map for sales, dispatch and local operations.

How it works

Search for a city or place, adjust the center and radius, then add as many zones as you need. Coordinates and labels remain in your browser. You can copy structured map data or download an SVG for a presentation, landing page or internal plan.

Why browser-based mapping?

This tool is designed for quick, low-friction planning. There is no account, database or complicated GIS dashboard. It is useful when a team needs a clear service-area decision in minutes rather than a full enterprise mapping system.
